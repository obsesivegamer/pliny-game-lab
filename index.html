Sheet A-1 · General plan

The first encyclopedia,
rebuilt as games.

Pliny the Elder tried to write down everything: volcanoes, bees, siege engines, the stars. Here his Naturalis Historia becomes 50 small games and simulations, shelved in 10 pavilions around one atrium. Each runs in your browser, and every one was designed and built by AI agents.
